Analyse

Interactions
What a ligand — or a chain — makes with the protein, from the distances and angles in the file: hydrogen bonds, hydrophobic contacts, π-stacking, cation–π, halogen bonds, metals. Salt bridges are found between chains: a ligand's charges are not in the file.
Reachable lysines
For a degrader in a ternary complex: the target's lysines that are on the surface and face the ligase. Geometry of this one pose — which lysine is ubiquitinated also depends on the E2 and on how the complex moves.
Clashes and contacts
Atoms of two parts that overlap: a clash when their van der Waals spheres overlap by 0.6 Å or more (drawn in red), a contact from −0.4 Å.
Pockets
Buried cavities in the chains shown, where a small molecule could sit: empty points with protein around them in most directions. Click one to see it alone and select the residues that line it.
AlphaFold error map
Measure
Press Pick atoms (or M), then click two atoms. Side-chain atoms need the Stick style, or the residue drawn as sticks — a residue you measure is drawn that way for you.
Interface
The residues of two chains that touch: any atom of one within the distance of any atom of the other.
Compare
Lay a second structure over this one. It is moved onto this one by the residues two chains have in common (a sequence alignment, then a rigid fit that leaves out the outliers).
