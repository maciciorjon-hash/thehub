How the drill-in works
A card is a place, not a link. Projects opens its folders in place, then that folder's experiments, with a breadcrumb back — two levels of the current tree, without leaving the home. You only switch screens when you open an actual entry, and then you get today's three-pane editor unchanged. This week and Running experiments behave the same way: a day expands to its steps, an experiment expands to its remaining steps with snooze and plate map in reach.
What this replaces: Labbook opens on "Nothing open — pick an entry on the left", with Today, Week planner and Projects as equal leaves in a text tree. Nothing on that screen says what is running, what is late, or what came back from Echo.
